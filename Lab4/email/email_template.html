⚠️ PHISHING SIMULATION — sample lure email for a cybersecurity course. Fictional brand. Not sent to anyone. Do not act on it.
😊 Friendzy
From: Friendzy Security <security@friendzy-support-verify.com>
To: undisclosed-recipients
Subject: ⚠️ Unusual login detected — verify within 24 hours

We noticed a suspicious sign-in to your account

Dear user,

Someone just tried to log in to your Friendzy account from a new device in another country. For your protection we have temporarily limited your account.

You must verify your identity within 24 hours or your account will be permanently deleted.

Verify my account now

Link points to: http://friendzy-support-verify.com/login   (hover reveals it is NOT friendzy.com)

Thank you,
The Friendzy Security Team

This is a fictional message created for training. Friendzy is not a real company.

🔍 Red flags in this email (for the debrief)

  1. Sender Look-alike domain friendzy-support-verify.com instead of the real domain.
  2. Greeting "Dear user" — a real service uses your name.
  3. Urgency A 24-hour deadline and a threat to delete your account pressure you into acting without thinking.
  4. Link The button text says "verify" but the URL goes to an attacker-controlled look-alike, not the official site.
  5. Channel Legitimate providers ask you to log in by typing the address yourself, not by clicking an email link.
